# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's project management guidance for cross-functional projects, from validating an initiative through delivery, release, and continuous improvement.

## Process overview

OctoAcme begins with **initiation**: confirm the business need and measurable outcomes, align stakeholders, identify resources and initial risks, and decide whether to proceed to planning. During **planning**, the team turns the approved initiative into an actionable backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, ownership, a Definition of Done, milestones, and dependencies.

In **execution**, teams deliver iterative, shippable increments and track work on a project board. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; the Product Manager/Product Lead defines outcomes, priorities, and success measures; developers build and test; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Risks and dependencies are assigned owners and mitigations, reviewed regularly, and escalated as needed.

Communication includes delivery-team standups, weekly PM and product/delivery syncs, stakeholder updates, and demos at sprint or milestone ends. Quality practices include unit and applicable integration tests, CI tests and linting, security scanning, manual QA when needed, and smoke tests for critical flows before release. Releases require acceptance criteria to be met, passing CI and security checks, release notes, and a rollback or mitigation plan; teams verify deployments and communicate outcomes. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents capture learnings and turn a few prioritized improvements into owned, trackable actions.
